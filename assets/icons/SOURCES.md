<https://github.com/google/material-design-icons>, licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (see `LICENSE`).
They were downloaded from
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/material-design-icons/master/png/<category>/<name>/materialicons/48dp/2x/baseline_<name>_black_48dp.png`
and recolored to white on transparent (RGBA, original alpha kept).
